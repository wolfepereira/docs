## Alpha Certified Expert: AI Governance

ACE.AI is the advanced tier of Alpha's certification program. It validates deep AI governance expertise suitable for advisory board chairs, AI ethics leads, search firm partners, and governance consultants.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|------------|--------|
| AGIQ Score | 160+ (Expert band) |
| Assessment | Extended psychometric assessment, proctored |
| Duration | Approximately 120 minutes |
| Fee | $6,500 |
| Prerequisite | ACD.AI certification or equivalent experience |
| Renewal | Annual recertification required |

## What Expert-Level Means

The Expert band (160-200) requires demonstrated ability to:

- Design governance frameworks, not just evaluate them
- Navigate cross-jurisdictional regulatory complexity (EU AI Act + NIST + ISO 42001 simultaneously)
- Apply governance principles to novel scenarios (agentic AI, autonomous systems, frontier models)
- Identify governance failures from limited evidence and recommend structural remediation
- Advise boards on governance strategy, not just compliance

## Expert Privileges

Beyond all ACD.AI benefits:

- **Rating Committee eligibility:** ACE.AI holders may be invited to serve on the Alpha Rating Committee
- **Board Brief contributor:** Invited to write for Alpha's governance newsletter
- **Board Red Team facilitator:** Qualified to lead Board Red Team simulation exercises
- **Faculty pipeline:** Priority consideration for Alpha Institute teaching and curriculum development
- **Enhanced registry listing:** Featured in the Expert tier of the Director Registry

## Progression Path

```
ACD.AI (AGIQ 120+)
    |
    ├── Continue Alpha Council engagement
    ├── Complete advanced governance modules
    ├── Accumulate board-level AI governance experience
    |
    └── ACE.AI Assessment (AGIQ 160+)
```

## Verification

Same API endpoint as ACD.AI:

```bash
curl https://api.alpha.ac/functions/v1/api-gateway/v1/verify/{credential_id}
```

The response distinguishes between ACD.AI and ACE.AI credential types.
