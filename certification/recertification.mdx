## Why Annual Recertification

AI governance is evolving rapidly. The EU AI Act enforcement begins August 2, 2026. New regulatory frameworks emerge quarterly. Board directors need current competency, not a one-time credential.

Annual recertification ensures that certified directors maintain active, relevant governance knowledge.

## Requirements

| Credential | Recertification Fee | Requirements |
|-----------|-------------------|--------------|
| ACD.AI | $600/year | 20 CPE credits + abbreviated assessment |
| ACE.AI | $900/year | 30 CPE credits + abbreviated assessment |

## Continuing Professional Education (CPE) Credits

Credits can be earned through:

| Activity | Credits |
|----------|---------|
| Alpha Council monthly sessions | 1 per session (12/year max) |
| Quarterly emerging tech briefings | 2 per briefing (8/year max) |
| Board Red Team exercise (facilitator) | 5 per exercise |
| Board Red Team exercise (participant) | 3 per exercise |
| Alpha Institute published contribution | 5 per article |
| Approved external governance education | Varies (submit for review) |

## Abbreviated Assessment

The recertification assessment is shorter than the initial certification (45 minutes) and focuses on:

- Regulatory updates since last certification
- New governance frameworks and standards
- Emerging technology governance scenarios (agentic AI, autonomous systems)
- Applied case studies from the past year

## Grace Period

Certified directors have a 90-day grace period after their annual renewal date. During the grace period:

- Credential remains valid but marked as "Renewal Pending"
- Public registry shows renewal status
- After 90 days without renewal, credential status changes to "Expired"

## Reinstatement

Expired credentials can be reinstated within 12 months by completing the full recertification requirements plus a reinstatement fee. After 12 months, a full re-assessment is required.
