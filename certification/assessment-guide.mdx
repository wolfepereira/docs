## Assessment Overview

The AGIQ assessment is a psychometrically validated evaluation of AI governance competency. It is not a memorization test. It evaluates judgment, decision-making, and applied knowledge.

## Format

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Duration** | 90 minutes (ACD.AI) / 120 minutes (ACE.AI) |
| **Format** | Scenario-based questions, governance analysis, regulatory application |
| **Proctoring** | Online proctored |
| **Scoring** | 0-200 scale across 6 dimensions |
| **Results** | Available within 5 business days |

## Question Types

### Scenario Analysis
You are presented with a governance situation and asked to identify risks, recommend actions, and prioritize responses. These test decision velocity and governance literacy.

### Regulatory Application
Given a company profile and regulatory requirement, map governance controls to specific articles/clauses. Tests regulatory navigation and technical fluency.

### Stakeholder Impact Assessment
Evaluate the governance implications of an AI deployment across multiple stakeholder groups. Tests stakeholder awareness and strategic integration.

### Board Simulation
Role-play a board discussion where you must ask the right questions, identify governance gaps, and propose committee-level actions. Tests all six dimensions.

- NIST AI RMF (GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, MANAGE functions)
- ISO 42001 (Clauses 4-10, Annex B)

## Scoring

Each of the six AGIQ dimensions is scored independently. Your composite AGIQ score is the weighted sum.

| Dimension | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Governance Literacy | 20% |
| Technical Fluency | 18% |
| Decision Velocity | 15% |
| Regulatory Navigation | 18% |
| Stakeholder Awareness | 14% |
| Strategic Integration | 15% |

Candidates scoring below the certification threshold (120 for ACD.AI, 160 for ACE.AI) receive a personalized development roadmap identifying specific dimensions to strengthen.

## Retake Policy

Candidates may retake the assessment after a 90-day waiting period. There is no limit on retake attempts, but each attempt requires the full assessment fee.
