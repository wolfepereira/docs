## Alpha Certified Director: AI Governance

ACD.AI validates that a board director or executive has demonstrated assessed competency in AI governance across the six AGIQ dimensions.

### What Makes ACD.AI Different

| Feature | ACD.AI | Generalist Credentials (NACD.DC, etc.) |
|---------|--------|----------------------------------------|
| **Focus** | AI governance specifically | General directorship competency |
| **Scoring** | Quantified AGIQ score (0-200) | Pass/fail binary |
| **Intelligence integration** | Linked to Alpha Radar, AGR, GMI | Standalone credential |
| **Regulatory specificity** | Standards Crosswalk (EU AI Act, NIST, ISO 42001) | General regulatory awareness |

ACD.AI is additive: "You have your NACD.DC. ACD.AI proves you can govern AI specifically."

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|------------|--------|
| AGIQ Score | 120+ (Advanced band) |
| Assessment | Psychometrically validated, proctored |
| Duration | Approximately 90 minutes |
| Fee | $3,500 |
| Renewal | Annual recertification required |

## AGIQ Assessment Dimensions

The assessment evaluates competency across six dimensions:

1. **Governance Literacy:** AI governance frameworks, board oversight responsibilities
2. **Technical Fluency:** Evaluating AI capabilities, limitations, and risks
3. **Decision Velocity:** Governance decisions under time pressure
4. **Regulatory Navigation:** EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001 requirements
5. **Stakeholder Awareness:** Workforce, community, and environmental impact analysis
6. **Strategic Integration:** Connecting AI governance to business strategy

## Credential Benefits

- **Post-nominal:** ACD.AI (used after name in professional contexts)
- **Public registry:** Verified at [alpha.ac/verify](https://alpha.ac/verify) and via API
- **Board visibility:** Listed in the Alpha Director Registry, searchable by Nominating/Governance Committees and executive search firms
- **Alpha Council access:** Complimentary first year of Council membership ($4,800 value)
- **Continuing education:** Access to quarterly emerging technology briefings

## Pathway to Certification

```
AGIQ Diagnostic (free, 15 min)
    |
    ├── Score 130+  →  Ready for full assessment
    ├── Score 60-129 →  Alpha Council Fluency program → Reassess
    └── Score 0-59   →  Agentic AI IQ explainers → Fluency → Reassess
```

## Verification API

Credentials can be verified programmatically:

```bash
curl https://api.alpha.ac/functions/v1/api-gateway/v1/verify/{credential_id}
```

Returns credential status, holder name, issue date, and expiration. No authentication required.
