## Scores

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **AGR** | Alpha Governance Rating. Letter-grade assessment (AAA-D) of a company's AI governance posture across 12 dimensions. |
| **GMI** | Governance Materiality Index. Quantifies how material AI governance risk is to a specific company (1-100). |
| **AGIQ** | Alpha Governance IQ. Measures individual and organizational AI governance readiness (0-200). |
| **GEH** | Governance Event Horizon. 12-month governance failure probability (0-100%). Always reported with confidence interval. |
| **DPD** | Disclosure-Practice Divergence. Gap between what a company says and what evidence shows (-100 to +100). |
| **GST** | Governance Stress Test. Regulatory scenario exposure rating (Critical/Elevated/Manageable/Prepared). |
| **AGR-A** | Agentic Deployment Rating. AI agent deployment governance assessment (AAA-D). |
| **AART** | Agentic AI Risk Tier. Tier 1-4 risk classification for companies deploying autonomous AI agents. |

## Framework

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **The Alpha Standard** | The 12-dimension governance assessment framework (current: v4). |
| **D2R Map** | Dimension-to-Regulation Map. Mandatory mapping of each dimension score to specific regulatory articles (EU AI Act, ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF). |
| **Rating Profile** | Developer, Deployer, or Hybrid. Determines how dimension weights are applied based on a company's AI business model. |
| **Alpha Trajectory** | Direction of governance posture: Advancing, Stable, or Declining. |
| **Alpha Watch** | Positive Watch (potential upgrade), Negative Watch (potential downgrade), or None. |
| **Concern Ceiling** | Mechanism that caps overall grade when a critical dimension falls below threshold. |
| **Corrigibility Event** | Documented instance where governance overrode a capability deployment or commercial decision. Highest-weight D12 evidence. |
| **Standards Crosswalk** | Mapping between Alpha's 12 dimensions and regulatory requirements (EU AI Act, ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF). |
