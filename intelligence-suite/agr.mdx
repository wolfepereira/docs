## Overview

The Alpha Governance Rating (AGR) is the core product of the Alpha Intelligence Suite. It provides a letter-grade assessment (AAA-D) of a company's AI governance posture across 12 dimensions.

AGR functions as a credit rating analog for AI governance: independent, continuous, evidence-based, and designed for fiduciary decision-making.

## What AGR Measures

AGR answers one question: **Is this company governing AI in a way that protects stakeholders and enables sustainable competitive advantage?**

It measures governance effectiveness across:
- Board oversight and accountability (D1)
- Safety testing and deployment controls (D2)
- Transparency and disclosure quality (D3)
- Data ethics and privacy practices (D4)
- Governance infrastructure maturity (D5)
- Fairness and bias mitigation (D6)
- Workforce and environmental impact management (D7)
- Regulatory preparedness (D8)
- Cybersecurity resilience (D9)
- Third-party and supply chain governance (D10)
- External stakeholder impact accountability (D11)
- System corrigibility and human oversight (D12)

## AGR Output Format

Every AGR output includes:

| Field | Example | Description |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| Grade | `BBB` | Letter grade on the Alpha Governance Scale |
| Composite Score | `72.4` | Weighted score across all 12 dimensions (0-100) |
| Trajectory | `Advancing` | Direction of governance posture change |
| Watch Status | `None` | Positive Watch, Negative Watch, or None |
| Confidence Score | `0.78` | Confidence level based on evidence density |
| Methodology Version | `2.0` | Version of The Alpha Standard applied |
| D2R Map | `[{...}]` | Dimension-to-Regulation mapping with specific citations |
