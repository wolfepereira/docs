## Overview

AGIQ measures AI governance competency at the individual director and organizational level. While AGR rates companies, AGIQ rates the people responsible for governing AI.

AGIQ is the foundation for:
- **ACD.AI certification** (Alpha Certified Director: AI Governance)
- **ACE.AI certification** (Alpha Certified Expert: AI Governance)
- **OGA** (Organizational Governance Assessment): aggregate AGIQ across all board members and executives

## Score Scale

| Score | Band | Label | Certification |
|-------|------|-------|--------------|
| 160-200 | Expert | Alpha Certified Expert | ACE.AI issued |
| 120-159 | Advanced | Alpha Certified Director | ACD.AI issued |
| 80-119 | Competent | Developing | Development roadmap issued |
| 40-79 | Emerging | Foundational | Full curriculum recommended |
| 0-39 | Developing | Pre-Foundational | Foundational program required |

Everyone who takes the AGIQ assessment receives a score and a personalized development roadmap. There is no "fail."

## Six AGIQ Dimensions

| Dimension | What It Measures |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Governance Literacy** | Understanding of AI governance frameworks, regulatory landscape, and board oversight responsibilities |
| **Technical Fluency** | Ability to evaluate AI system capabilities, limitations, and risk profiles without being a technologist |
| **Decision Velocity** | Speed and quality of governance decisions in time-pressured scenarios |
| **Regulatory Navigation** | Knowledge of EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, and cross-jurisdictional requirements |
| **Stakeholder Awareness** | Ability to identify and weigh impacts on employees, communities, and external stakeholders |
| **Strategic Integration** | Connecting AI governance to business strategy, competitive positioning, and long-term value creation |

## Entry Point: AGIQ Diagnostic

The [AGIQ Diagnostic](https://alpha.ac/institute/agiq-diagnostic) is a free, 15-minute assessment that provides a directional score and routes individuals to the appropriate tier:

| Directional Score | Recommended Path |
|------------------|-----------------|
| 0-59 | Start with Alpha Agentic AI IQ (free explainers) |
| 60-129 | Join Alpha Council Fluency program |
| 130-200 | Ready for full psychometric AGIQ assessment |

## OGA: Organizational Governance Assessment

OGA aggregates individual AGIQ scores across a company's board and executive team to produce an organizational governance readiness score. This is the primary enterprise product built on AGIQ.

OGA enables:
- Board composition gap analysis for Nominating/Governance Committees
- Director education budget prioritization
- Board refresh planning with AI governance competency as a criterion
- Peer board benchmarking on governance readiness

## API Access

AGIQ data is accessible through the Director endpoint:

```bash
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
  https://api.alpha.ac/functions/v1/api-gateway/v1/directors/{director_id}/agiq
```
