## Overview

The Governance Materiality Index (GMI) answers a different question than AGR. While AGR measures governance quality, GMI measures governance **relevance**: how much does AI governance matter to this specific company?

A regional bank deploying a single chatbot has low AI governance materiality. A frontier model developer or a healthcare company deploying clinical AI has high materiality. GMI quantifies this difference.

## Score Range

| Range | Materiality Level | Implication |
|-------|------------------|-------------|
| 80-100 | **Critical** | AI governance is existential to business continuity. Board-level priority. |
| 60-79 | **High** | AI governance materially affects risk profile and regulatory exposure. |
| 40-59 | **Moderate** | AI governance is relevant but not dominant in the company's risk landscape. |
| 20-39 | **Low** | Limited AI deployment. Governance requirements are emerging, not urgent. |
| 1-19 | **Minimal** | AI is not material to current operations. Monitoring-only posture appropriate. |

## Inputs

GMI is calculated from:

- **AI deployment intensity:** Number and criticality of AI systems in production
- **Sector AI materiality:** Industry-level AI adoption and risk patterns
- **Revenue dependency:** Percentage of revenue tied to AI-enabled products or services
- **Regulatory exposure:** Jurisdictional requirements (EU AI Act high-risk classification, etc.)
- **Workforce AI impact:** Degree of workforce transformation driven by AI
- **Public scrutiny:** Media and stakeholder attention to the company's AI activities

## How GMI Relates to AGR

GMI contextualizes AGR. A company with:
- **High GMI + Low AGR** = Critical governance gap. Immediate risk.
- **High GMI + High AGR** = Well-governed in a high-stakes environment.
- **Low GMI + Low AGR** = Low priority. Monitor for materiality changes.
- **Low GMI + High AGR** = Over-invested in governance relative to exposure (rare but possible).

## API Access

GMI is included in every ratings API response alongside AGR:

```json
{
  "ticker": "AAPL",
  "agr": { "grade": "BBB", "score": 72.4 },
  "gmi": { "score": 78, "level": "High" }
}
```
