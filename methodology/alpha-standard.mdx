The Alpha Standard is the comprehensive framework used to assess AI governance across all rated companies. It has evolved through four versions as the field has matured.

## Version History

| Version | Dimensions | Added | Rationale |
|---------|-----------|-------|-----------|
| v1 | D1-D8 | Original 8 | Core governance dimensions covering leadership through regulatory compliance |
| v2 | D1-D10 | D9 Cybersecurity, D10 Supply Chain | AI systems create novel attack surfaces and vendor dependencies |
| v3 | D1-D11 | D11 Stakeholder Impact | External harm accountability: workforce displacement, environmental impact, societal effects |
| v4 | D1-D12 | D12 Corrigibility | Demonstrated ability to override, shut down, or correct AI systems under pressure |

## Scoring Mechanics

Each dimension is scored 0-100 based on codified indicators. The composite score is a weighted sum based on the company's Rating Profile (Developer, Deployer, or Hybrid).

## Cross-Dimension Checks

The Alpha Standard includes several cross-cutting mechanisms:

- **Concern Ceiling Logic:** The independent concern screen can cap the overall grade at D, CCC, BB, or A regardless of the composite.
- **Critical Floor Checks:** General floors apply across D1-D10, with added universal floors for D1 and D8, profile-specific floors for D2, D6, D9, and D10, and assessed-rating D12 floors for corrigibility.
- **Cross-Signal Inference:** Signals detected in one dimension can affect scores in related dimensions. A cybersecurity incident (D9) that reveals poor incident response also impacts D2 (Safety) and D8 (Regulatory).
- **Committee Zone Coherence:** High variance between dimensions within the same committee zone flags structural governance gaps.

## Regulatory Alignment

Every dimension maps to specific regulatory requirements through the D2R Map (Dimension-to-Regulation Map):

- **EU AI Act** articles and annexes
- **ISO 42001** clauses
- **NIST AI RMF** functions and categories

The D2R Map is mandatory on every rating output, ensuring that governance scores translate directly into regulatory compliance posture.
