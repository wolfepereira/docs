## Committee Zones

The 12 dimensions are not independent scoring lanes. They cluster into 5 committee accountability zones, reflecting how governance actually works at the board level.

A company's governance score is 5 committee accountability zones, each of which must demonstrate coherent oversight.

| Committee Zone | Dimensions | Zone Weight | Focus |
|---------------|-----------|-------------|-------|
| **Full Board** | D1 (Leadership), D11 (Stakeholder Impact) | 15% | AI governance at the highest level, external accountability |
| **Risk Committee** | D2 (Safety), D4 (Data Ethics), D9 (Cybersecurity), D12 (Corrigibility) | 30% | Safety, data, security resilience, system controllability |
| **Audit Committee** | D3 (Transparency), D8 (Regulatory), D10 (Supply Chain) | 25% | Disclosure, compliance, vendor governance |
| **Compensation/HR** | D6 (Fairness), D7 (Workforce & Society) | 15% | Bias mitigation and workforce impact |
| **Nominating/Governance** | D5 (Governance Infrastructure) | 15% | Policies, architecture, board composition |

## Why This Matters

No new AI committees are needed. Existing board structures already have the jurisdiction to oversee AI governance. The mapping shows which committee owns each dimension.

### Zone Coherence Signal

High variance between dimensions within the same committee zone flags a structural governance gap:

- If D3 (Transparency) scores 80 but D8 (Regulatory) scores 40, the **Audit Committee** is not providing coherent oversight.
- If D2 (Safety) scores 75 but D9 (Cybersecurity) scores 30, the **Risk Committee** has a blind spot.
- If D6 (Fairness) scores 70 but D7 (Workforce Impact) scores 25, the **Compensation/HR Committee** is addressing bias but ignoring workforce transition.

Zone coherence flags appear in every rating report.

## Practical Application

### For Board Chairs
Each committee receives a zone score alongside the overall company rating. This enables targeted governance improvement without requiring a full board overhaul.

### For Committee Chairs
Dimension scores within your zone identify specific gaps. A Risk Committee Chair seeing strong D2 but weak D12 knows to focus on corrigibility and override mechanisms.

### For Corporate Secretaries
The committee mapping provides the governance calendar structure: each committee reviews its zone dimensions at the cadence appropriate to the risk level.
