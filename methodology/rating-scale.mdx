## Alpha Governance Scale

Public Profiles do not display public letter grades. The scale below applies to Assessed Ratings, committee-reviewed internal grading, and any context where Alpha is explicitly issuing a full AGR grade.

| Grade | Tier | Composite Score | Description |
|-------|------|----------------|-------------|
| **AAA** | Leaders | 93.0-100.0 | Governance is demonstrably market-leading, stress-tested, and hard to game. |
| **AA** | Leaders | 85.0-92.99 | Governance is comprehensive, well evidenced, and consistently effective. |
| **A** | Governance-Ready | 77.0-84.99 | Governance is strong, but not yet leader-level across the full framework. |
| **BBB** | Governance-Ready | 70.0-76.99 | Governance meets an institutional baseline with visible gaps still to close. |
| **BB** | Developing | 62.0-69.99 | Governance architecture exists, but execution and proof remain uneven. |
| **B** | Developing | 54.0-61.99 | Basic governance is present, but important controls are weak, partial, or unproven. |
| **CCC** | Laggards | 46.0-53.99 | Governance is reactive and materially incomplete. |
| **CC** | Laggards | 35.0-45.99 | Governance is very weak, with significant exposure across core dimensions. |
| **C** | Laggards | 25.0-34.99 | Governance exists mostly in name only. |
| **D** | Disqualified | 0.0-24.99 | Governance failure, severe deficiency, or disqualifying concern. |
| **NR** | Not Rated | - | Coverage not initiated, insufficient evidence, rating withdrawn/suspended, entity request, or conflict of interest. |

## Display Format

Ratings are displayed with trajectory and, where available, the Governance Event Horizon:

```
AGR: B / GEH: 67% (+/-12pp, 90% CI)
```

## Trajectory

Every rated company receives a trajectory indicator reflecting the direction of governance posture over the most recent assessment period:

| Trajectory | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Advancing** | Governance posture is improving. Evidence of new controls, stronger oversight, or resolved gaps. |
| **Stable** | Governance posture is consistent. No material change in either direction. |
| **Declining** | Governance posture is weakening. New risks emerging, controls degrading, or oversight gaps widening. |

## Watch Status

A watch flag signals that a rating is under review due to a material event:

| Status | Trigger |
|--------|---------|
| **Positive Watch** | Evidence suggests an upcoming upgrade (new governance initiative, regulatory achievement, leadership appointment). |
| **Negative Watch** | Evidence suggests a potential downgrade (incident, regulatory action, leadership departure, disclosure gap). |
| **None** | No material events pending. |

## Floors and Ceilings

A critical failure can cap the overall grade regardless of composite score. The current methodology applies the following hard constraints:

- Any applicable D1-D10 dimension below 30/100: grade capped at D
- Any applicable D1-D10 dimension at 30-39/100: grade capped at CC
- Any applicable D1-D10 dimension at 40-49/100: grade capped at B
- D1 (Leadership) below 50/100: grade capped at A
- D8 (Regulatory) below 40/100: grade capped at BBB
- For Assessed Ratings, D12 (Corrigibility) at 0: grade capped at B, and D12 below 20/100: grade capped at BB
- Concern screen results can also cap the grade independently: one Elevated concern caps at A, two Elevated concerns cap at BB, one Severe caps at CCC, and one Disqualifying concern caps at D

This ensures that a company cannot achieve a high overall rating while carrying a critical governance blind spot. The full scoring logic is documented in the [Scoring Rubric](/methodology/rubric).
