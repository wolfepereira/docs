## Alpha Governance Scale

| Grade | Tier | Composite Score | Description |
|-------|------|----------------|-------------|
| **AAA** | Leaders | 95-100 | Exceptional governance across all dimensions. Industry benchmark. |
| **AA** | Leaders | 88-94 | Very strong governance with minor gaps. Board-level commitment evident. |
| **A** | Leaders | 80-87 | Strong governance with clear accountability structures and proactive posture. |
| **BBB** | Governance-Ready | 70-79 | Adequate governance. Meets baseline regulatory expectations. Some dimensions underdeveloped. |
| **BB** | Governance-Ready | 60-69 | Developing governance. Framework exists but execution gaps are present. |
| **B** | Developing | 50-59 | Basic governance. Policies exist but lack specificity, testing, or board oversight. |
| **CCC** | Developing | 40-49 | Weak governance. Significant gaps across multiple dimensions. Regulatory risk elevated. |
| **CC** | Laggards | 30-39 | Very weak governance. Minimal evidence of structured AI oversight. |
| **C** | Laggards | 20-29 | Near-absent governance. Critical dimensions unaddressed. |
| **D** | Laggards | 0-19 | No meaningful AI governance detected or active governance failure on record. |
| **NR** | Not Rated | - | Coverage not initiated, insufficient evidence, rating withdrawn/suspended, entity request, or conflict of interest. |

## Display Format

Ratings are displayed with trajectory and, where available, the Governance Event Horizon:

```
AGR: B / GEH: 67% (+/-12pp, 90% CI)
```

## Trajectory

Every rated company receives a trajectory indicator reflecting the direction of governance posture over the most recent assessment period:

| Trajectory | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Advancing** | Governance posture is improving. Evidence of new controls, stronger oversight, or resolved gaps. |
| **Stable** | Governance posture is consistent. No material change in either direction. |
| **Declining** | Governance posture is weakening. New risks emerging, controls degrading, or oversight gaps widening. |

## Watch Status

A watch flag signals that a rating is under review due to a material event:

| Status | Trigger |
|--------|---------|
| **Positive Watch** | Evidence suggests an upcoming upgrade (new governance initiative, regulatory achievement, leadership appointment). |
| **Negative Watch** | Evidence suggests a potential downgrade (incident, regulatory action, leadership departure, disclosure gap). |
| **None** | No material events pending. |

## Concern Ceiling Logic

A critical failure in a high-weight dimension can cap the overall grade regardless of composite score. For example:

- D12 (Corrigibility) score below 20: grade capped at BB
- D2 (Safety) score below 15 for Developer profile: grade capped at B
- D8 (Regulatory) with active enforcement action: automatic Negative Watch

This ensures that a company cannot achieve a high overall rating while having a critical governance blind spot.
