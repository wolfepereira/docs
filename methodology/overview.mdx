Alpha Governance Ratings provide independent, evidence-based assessments of how companies govern artificial intelligence. The rating system functions as a credit rating analog for AI governance, designed for board directors, institutional investors, C-suite executives, and governance professionals making fiduciary decisions.

## The Question We Answer

**Is this company governing AI in a way that protects stakeholders and enables sustainable competitive advantage?**

We measure governance effectiveness, not AI capability or strategy quality. We measure outcomes and stress responses, not just governance artifacts. A published policy earns partial credit. Evidence that the policy was applied in a real decision earns full credit. Evidence that governance held under competitive pressure earns the highest marks.

## The 4-Phase Alpha Standard

Every rating follows a structured, repeatable process:

| Phase | Name | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| 1 | **Evidence Collection** | Gather public filings (10-K, DEF 14A, proxy statements), corporate disclosures, safety reports, news coverage, and proprietary signals from the Alpha Governance Knowledge Graph |
| 2 | **Dimension Scoring** | Score each of the 12 dimensions (D1-D12) using codified indicators, weighted by the company's Rating Profile |
| 3 | **Cross-Dimension Analysis** | Apply concern ceiling logic, critical floor checks, cross-signal inference, and committee zone coherence analysis |
| 4 | **Grade Assignment** | Calculate composite score, assign letter grade (AAA-D), determine trajectory and watch status |

## Rating Profiles

Different companies face different governance challenges. Alpha uses three profiles within the same dimensional framework:

| Profile | Description | Higher Weight On |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Developer** | Companies that build, train, and distribute AI models | Safety (D2), Data Ethics (D4), Cybersecurity (D9) |
| **Deployer** | Companies that integrate, deploy, and operate AI systems | Governance Infrastructure (D5), Fairness (D6), Workforce Impact (D7), Supply Chain (D10) |
| **Hybrid** | Companies that both build and deploy | Blended weights across all dimensions |

The rating report always discloses which profile was applied and why.

## Evidence Tiers

All evidence is classified by reliability:

| Tier | Source Type | Weight Multiplier |
|------|-----------|------------------|
| T1 | Regulatory actions, enforcement, court filings | 3x |
| T2 | Verified internal documents, audit reports | 3x |
| T3 | Expert assessments, structured interviews | 3x |
| T4 | Proprietary signal analysis (AGKG) | 3x |
| T5 | Public disclosures, news, corporate websites | 1x |

A rating that relies solely on T5 signals is flagged with data tier limitations in the output.

## Independence

Alpha rates companies on public data without seeking permission or offering courtesy reviews. We do not partner with rated entities. Independence creates credibility. When a company has an active paid advisory engagement, the rating requires Rating Committee review with conflict disclosure on record.
